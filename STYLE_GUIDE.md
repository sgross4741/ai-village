# My AI Village — Style Guide

This paragraph goes word for word into every art prompt.

---

16-bit SNES-era pixel art for a cozy, original village game, shown in a top-down three-quarter view. Warm color palette of golden ochre, terracotta red, honey-brown wood, soft sage and moss green, and creamy stone, with only 3–4 shading tones per material. A single light source from the upper left, so highlights sit on top-left edges and shadows fall toward the lower right. Crisp dark outlines around every object and character, clean hard-edged pixels, no blur, no anti-aliasing, no smooth gradients, no photorealism. Consistent scale on a 16×16-pixel tile grid. No text anywhere: no letters, numbers, words on signs, logos, or watermarks.
