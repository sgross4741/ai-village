# The Village Library

Put reference files here for the town to use: your offers and prices, your brand voice,
templates, a short "about my business", checklists. Plain text works best:
`.md`, `.txt` or `.csv` files (save a document as plain text, or paste it into a `.md` file).

Who reads the library (from your tool matrix): Augustus (Sales), Jo (Delivery), Ezra (Product),
Hattie (Client Success), Rufus (Marketing & Content) and Beatrice (Legal & Compliance).
The village helper adds these files to their instructions; nobody can change them.

This folder stays on your computer. Only this README is saved to GitHub.
Up to about 40,000 characters are used in total, in file-name order.
