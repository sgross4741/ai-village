# Skills for the village

Skills are written guides that make the townspeople and workers better at a kind of work.
The agents have no tools, so they can't open skills themselves: the village helper adds the
right guide to their instructions when the work calls for it.

| Skill | Used for | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `frontend-design/` | Websites and documents made in the Workshop (General Store) | Anthropic's public skills, https://github.com/anthropics/skills (Apache License 2.0, see its LICENSE.txt). Copied unchanged. |

To add another skill, put its folder here and ask Claude to wire it in.
